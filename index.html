Frequently Asked Questions

Is PureText Clean safe to use for sensitive data? ▼
Yes. PureText Clean is a browser-only tool. All processing happens locally on your machine via JavaScript — your text is never sent to a server, saved, or exposed to anyone.
How do I remove duplicate lines from a list online? ▼
Paste your text into the input field and click Remove Duplicates under the List Ordering section. All repeated lines are removed instantly, keeping the first occurrence.
Can I convert a comma-separated list into separate lines? ▼
Yes. The Comma → Lines tool splits any comma-separated values into individual lines. You can also do the reverse with Text → Comma.
Does PureText Clean work offline? ▼
Yes. Once the page loads, every tool runs entirely in your browser. You can disconnect from the internet and all features continue working without interruption.
How do I convert text to Title Case? ▼
Paste your text and click Title Case under Case Transformations. PureText Clean intelligently skips short conjunctions and prepositions like "and", "of", and "the" so your output looks professionally formatted.
Can I sort a list alphabetically for free? ▼
Yes, completely free. Paste your list and click Sort A → Z or Sort Z → A. Each line is treated as one item and sorted instantly with no limit on list size.
How do I strip extra spaces and blank lines from text? ▼
Use Strip Extra Spaces to collapse multiple spaces into one per line, and Remove Empty Lines to delete blank rows. You can apply both in sequence for a fully clean result.
Can I URL-encode or HTML-encode text? ▼
Yes. The Sanitize & Clean section includes URL Encode, URL Decode, HTML Encode, and HTML Decode — all processed locally in your browser.
Is there a keyboard shortcut to search for tools? ▼
Yes! Press Ctrl + K (or ⌘K on Mac) to jump straight to the search bar. Start typing and tools filter in real time.
Is PureText Clean completely free? ▼
Yes, 100% free — no sign-up, no account, no limits. The site is supported by non-intrusive Google AdSense advertising. If you find it useful, you can also buy us a coffee ☕.
